A RAG-powered knowledge system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation to turn your documents, PDFs, and business data into a searchable AI knowledge base. When a user asks a question, the system retrieves the most relevant documents using vector search (FAISS) and passes them to a language model to generate an accurate, grounded answer — rather than relying on the model's training data alone.
An AI chatbot handles conversational interactions — answering questions based on your documents or data. A custom AI agent goes further: it can autonomously plan, make decisions, use tools, call APIs, and execute multi-step workflows without human intervention. Agents are built for automation and task execution; chatbots are built for conversation.
A basic AI chatbot or RAG system typically takes 1–2 weeks to build and deploy. A custom AI agent with multi-step workflows and integrations typically takes 2–4 weeks depending on complexity. Cloud deployment and testing are included in these timelines.
Imran deploys on AWS (Elastic Beanstalk, Lambda, ECR, S3, Amazon Bedrock) and GCP (Vertex AI, Cloud Run). All deployments use Docker containerization and FastAPI backends for scalable, production-ready infrastructure.

An AI voice agent receptionist is a custom 24/7 automated telephone system that answers your business calls, speaks naturally with low latency, books appointments directly into your calendar (Calendly, Google Calendar), qualifies leads by collecting caller details, forwards urgent calls to a human, and sends post-call summaries to email, Google Sheets, or your CRM. It is built with Vapi.ai or Retell AI, GPT-4o, Deepgram speech-to-text, and ElevenLabs text-to-speech — ideal for dental clinics, HVAC contractors, law firms, and real estate agencies.
